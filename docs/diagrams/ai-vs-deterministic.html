ios-xr-nettools · AI space vs deterministic space

The model's one decision

Not a spectrum — a narrow slot. Everything from the syslog line arriving to the deterministic descent producing a finding is code: parsed, gated, admitted, and stripped of every identifier before a token ever reaches a model. The model's only real decision is which tool to call, and for two of the four it is offered, there is nothing left to supply at all. That choice happens before the commands it triggers run, not after — the AI's turn is one narrow slot in the middle of a deterministic run, not a phase bolted onto the end of one.

Where this actually runs

One physical host carries two independent container layers — a containerlab fabric of 9 IOS-XR routers plus 4 CE traffic generators, and a 24-service Docker Compose platform (14 running now). ollama is the only process holding the machine's NVIDIA RTX A4000 (16376 MiB, driver 580.173.02) — a host-level systemd service outside both container layers; nothing in Compose requests the GPU. nettools-mcp also runs containerized as a bearer-token-authenticated, plain-HTTP transport published on 0.0.0.0:8000 for trusted LAN/VPN clients.

Data flow · ios-xr-nettools

What's permitted, produced, decided, and refused

Device show output only exists because the allowlist already approved it. The parser turns it into typed fields; the reducer may withhold some of them under budget — but withheld is recorded as withheld, never folded into a zero. The descent decides from what remains, and grounding is the last gate: its failure objects carry no field a model's prose could occupy, so a refused report cannot be emitted by forgetting to redact.

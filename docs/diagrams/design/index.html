Diagram set · ios-xr-nettools v1.2.0

Eleven views of a read-only network inspection tool

Hand-authored presentation diagrams covering the whole stack — the tool, the lab platform it runs beside, and the fabric it reads. Every figure in them was derived from the tree or measured against the running system, not recalled.

These are a presentation layer, not the record.

The nine SVGs in docs/diagrams/ are generated from the source tree and byte-pinned by tests/test_diagrams.py — they cannot drift from the code without failing CI, which is what makes them trustworthy and also what keeps them plain. These eleven are drawn by hand: better to read, and capable of going stale. When the two disagree, the generated ones are right.
